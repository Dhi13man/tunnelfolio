# Governance

Tunnelfolio is currently maintained by Dhiman Seal under a maintainer-led model.
The maintainer sets scope, reviews contributions, manages releases, and makes the
final decision when consensus is not reached.

Decisions favor a small security boundary, deterministic network behavior, and
operator clarity. Material public-behavior or trust-boundary changes should be
discussed in an issue before implementation. Governance may evolve if a durable
contributor community forms.
